Privacy Policy (English)

What we keep: a random device ID in your browser (to count limits and plans); if you sign in with Google, your name, email address and profile picture (to keep your plan and chats on every device; we never see your Google password); your chats in your browser, and if signed in, your latest 40 chats on our server; usage counts and total 👍/👎 counts.

Where data goes: your questions and attached photos/files are sent to our AI provider (Anthropic) to create answers; image descriptions (never your name or email) go to the Pollinations image service when you ask for pictures; some answers use web search. The site runs on Vercel and counts are stored in Upstash. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Your choices: sign out at any time; clearing browser data removes chats on that device; to delete your account and saved chats from our server, contact us and we will delete them.

Children: users under 13 should use Al Bayan AI with a parent's or guardian's permission.
